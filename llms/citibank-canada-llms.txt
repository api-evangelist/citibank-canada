# Citibank Canada

> Citibank Canada (Citi Canada) is the Canadian arm of Citigroup, chartered under Canada's Bank Act as a Schedule II (foreign bank subsidiary) deposit-taking institution, headquartered at Citigroup Place, 123 Front Street West, Toronto. It is an institutional and corporate bank only — securities trading, cash management, treasury, trade finance, custody, clearing, securities financing and private banking — having exited Canadian consumer retail banking (its MasterCard portfolio was sold to CIBC in 2010 and CitiFinancial Canada, now Fairstone, was divested in 2017). It is a member of the Canadian Bankers Association and the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation (CDIC).

## Notes

- Citi Canada runs no Canada-specific developer portal and publishes no first-party public API or downloadable OpenAPI/Swagger definition.
- The Canadian subdomains developer.citibank.ca and www.citibank.ca do not resolve (DNS/TLS failure).
- Corporate and institutional clients integrate through Citigroup's GROUP-LEVEL platforms — the Citi Developer Experience and the CitiConnect API platform (Treasury and Trade Solutions) — for payments, statements/reporting, balance inquiry, beneficiary/payee data and FX. Documentation and the sandbox / API Explorer sit behind client onboarding.
- Canada has no operational open-banking mandate. The federal Consumer-Driven Banking framework (Budget 2024 / FES 2024, overseen by the FCAC) is legislated but not yet live. Citi Canada holds no retail consumer accounts here, so consumer data-aggregation access is not applicable.

## APIs

- [Citi Institutional (CitiConnect) API Platform](https://developer.citi.com/apis/): Citigroup's group-level institutional API platform that Citi Canada's corporate and institutional clients integrate with for payments, statements and reporting, balance inquiry, beneficiary/payee data and FX. Documentation and sandbox sit behind client onboarding; no publicly downloadable spec.
